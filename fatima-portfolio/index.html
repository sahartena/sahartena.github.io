01 — Leadership Profile

Programs don't deliver themselves. Leaders do.

Fatima Elsheikh is a PMP®-certified ERP Delivery Lead with more than eight years of experience taking enterprise ERP implementations and digital transformation initiatives from business analysis and solution design through implementation, go-live, and hypercare — across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, and Sudan. Her career spans the full arc of delivery: developer, business analyst, senior functional consultant, project lead, and delivery lead — which means she governs programs with a working command of every layer beneath them.

She leads cross-functional teams of functional consultants, developers, QA engineers, and business stakeholders while owning scope, schedules, risks, budgets, and executive communications. Clients know her for composed, evidence-based governance: business workshops that surface the real requirement, GAP analysis that prevents scope surprises, and UAT discipline that makes go-live a milestone — not a gamble.

  • End-to-end delivery: discovery → design → build → UAT → go-live → hypercare
  • Executive-grade governance: scope, schedule, risk, budget, and stakeholder communications
  • Technical fluency: former Odoo developer and team lead — speaks the engineering language

01

Red Bull Mobile — Enterprise ERP Implementation

Telecom · Saudi Arabia · Appness Technology

  • End-to-end discovery → rollout
  • [95]% UAT pass rate at exit
  • [120]+ users onboarded

Challenge

A high-visibility telecom brand needed an end-to-end ERP implementation — from discovery through production rollout — under tight commercial timelines, with zero tolerance for disruption to live operations.

Approach

Fatima led the full lifecycle: business workshops and requirements gathering, GAP analysis and solution design, multi-team implementation coordination, then structured UAT, user training, and deployment planning — with delivery status reported directly to executive stakeholders throughout.

02

Muscat Stock Exchange — ERP for a National Market Institution

Financial services · Oman · Appness Technology

  • Regulated environment delivery
  • [100]% critical-path milestones met
  • [4] departments transformed

Challenge

A national stock exchange required an ERP implementation with the governance rigor of a regulated financial institution — precise scope control, auditable decisions, and stakeholders at the highest level of the organization.

Approach

She ran disciplined scope, schedule, priority, and risk management across the engagement — facilitating requirement workshops with exchange leadership, coordinating multi-field implementation teams, and holding a strict UAT and deployment-planning regime before any production change.

Result

Delivered the implementation through production rollout with training and hypercare, maintaining stakeholder confidence in a zero-surprise environment.

Cross-sector · Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Sudan

  • 4 countries
